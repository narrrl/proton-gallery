Proton Gallery for Windows includes parts of GStreamer 1.26.10 for video
playback: the GStreamer core and base libraries, a selection of its plugins,
and the libraries those plugins use (GLib, FFmpeg's libavcodec, libavformat
and libavutil, dav1d, libvpx, libopus, libogg, libvorbis, among others).

They are the unmodified binaries of GStreamer's official MinGW x86_64 build:

  https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org/data/pkg/windows/1.26.10/mingw/

GStreamer and GLib are licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License,
version 2.1 or later. FFmpeg's libraries as built by GStreamer are licensed
under the LGPL 2.1 or later. dav1d, libvpx, libopus, libogg and libvorbis are
under BSD-style licenses. The exact license of each library, and its source,
is published by the GStreamer project:

  https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org/download/
  https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/gstreamer/cerbero (the recipes for this build)

The libraries are loaded as separate DLLs from the bin and lib\gstreamer-1.0
folders; you may replace them with versions you build yourself.
